200m - The Epipelagic

The Sunlight Zone

The vibrant cradle of ocean life.

Spanning the top 200 meters, this zone absorbs over 90% of all solar energy. It is home to thriving coral reefs, vast kelp forests, and the vast majority of marine biodiversity.

1,000m - The Mesopelagic

The Twilight Zone

The realm of fading embers.

Sunlight rapidly dimishes to total darkness at 1,000 meters. Temperatures hover just above freezing. Here, creatures rely on incredible eyes and shadowy camouflage to survive the cold, pressurized depths.

4,000m - The Bathypelagic

The Midnight Zone

A sanctuary of cold fires.

Reaching down to 4,000 meters, this zone is completely devoid of solar light. The water pressure is crushing, and temperatures remain close to freezing. Organisms have developed stunning bioluminescent displays for hunting and communication.

11,000m - The Hadal Zone

The Hadal Trench

The absolute edge of the abyss.

Plunging over 11,000 meters down into the Mariana Trench. In this domain of ultimate silence and crushing weight, hydrothermal vents feed highly specialized ecosystems. You have successfully explored the deepest limits of planet Earth.
